# Privacy Policy — Zoom CheckList

**Last updated: October 10, 2026**

Zoom CheckList is an application designed to help educators and students manage attendance in online Zoom meetings.

## Information We Process

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, the application may process Zoom account information, meeting identifiers, participant display names, attendance status, and meeting join or leave times.

## How We Use Information

We use information only to support attendance tracking, classroom administration, reporting, and troubleshooting.

## Data Storage and Security

Information is stored and accessed only as needed to provide the application's features. We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

## Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to operate the service.

## Data Rights and Contact

Users may contact the application operator to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law.

Contact: Please use the support contact provided on the Zoom CheckList application listing.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change.
